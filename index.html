I am an independent researcher with a background in product design and frontend development. I study how AI systems preserve, retrieve, and use information across long interactions.

My work spans memory representation, retrieval, context construction, and the evidence needed to support reliable answers.

My current research develops verifiable evidence chains in long dialogues. The aim is to connect questions and answers through traceable facts, events, objects, and references in the source text. This includes a formal result that is being translated into Lean.

More broadly, I am interested in memory and long-context systems whose answers remain inspectable and grounded in their source material.

I am always open to research discussions and collaboration. Feel free to reach out by email.
